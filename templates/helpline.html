Cyber Emergency Tips

  • 🔵 Do not share your OTP, PIN, password or bank details with anyone.
  • 🔵 Do not click unknown links in SMS, emails or websites.
  • 🔵 Report the incident immediately to the helpline.
  • 🔵 Keep all proof like screenshots, messages, call logs, etc.
  • 🔵 Stay calm and act quickly to minimize the damage.

Need Immediate Assistance?

If you are a victim of cyber fraud, call 1930 or visit the nearest cyber crime police station.
